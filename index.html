Our Story

Integrated Farming.
Pure Excellence.

At CONC Dairy Farm, we believe we are the change we badly look for.

Founded in 2026, CONC is built on the foundation that sustainable agriculture and uncompromised purity can reshape our food systems. We raise superior dairy cattle, produce high-protein pasture feeds, and process rich artisan yogurt alongside a full line of premium fresh dairy products.

Every drop of milk and container of yogurt reflects our commitment to animal welfare, environmental stewardship, and health-first nutrition.
